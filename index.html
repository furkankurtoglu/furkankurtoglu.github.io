02

SCIENTIFIC AI & LARGE-SCALE SIMULATION

Developing neural-network surrogates for metabolic models and building 3D simulations with up to a million cell agents.

Neural-network surrogates are trained to approximate the outputs of computationally expensive metabolic models. The aim is to reduce the cost of repeated metabolic calculations while preserving the accuracy needed to study cell behavior.

In a three-dimensional agent-based simulation, each cell has its own state and behavior and interacts with its surroundings. Coupling these simulations with metabolic surrogates could help make it practical to represent metabolism across large cell populations.

  • Neural-Network
  • Surrogate Models
  • Agent-Based Modeling
  • 3D Simulation
  • 3D Visualization
03

MECHANISTIC SIMULATION

Multiscale modeling of intestinal crypt organization

A three-dimensional CompuCell3D research model coupling intracellular Delta–Notch signaling with neighboring-cell interactions, spatial WNT cues, proliferation, and epithelial differentiation. The simulation connects molecular states to cell behavior within a crypt geometry, including Paneth, goblet, and enterocyte cell types.

The implementation combines cell mechanics with per-cell SBML models and transfers intracellular model states during cell division. WNT is represented by a prescribed spatial profile.

  • CompuCell3D
  • Cellular Potts modeling
  • SBML / ODE integration
  • Python

03 / BACKGROUND

Academic background

Training across bioengineering, systems biology, and computational engineering.

I’m Furkan, a computational biologist and computational engineer. My work spans molecular mechanisms, cellular behavior, and the software that helps us study both.

At Indiana University’s MathCancer Lab, I worked on multiscale modeling and scientific software from 2018 to 2024. I earned my M.S. in Intelligent Systems Engineering at Indiana University Bloomington in 2024, building on bioengineering degrees from Montana State University and Istanbul Technical University, and graduate study in bioinformatics and systems biology at Gebze Technical University.

Today, I pursue independent research in digital twins and scientific AI alongside my work as AI Engineer and Operations Manager at Greywolf LLC.
